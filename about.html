About Us

Since 1971, Powering Progress with Precision.

Prakash Electronics Prakash Electronics, founded in 1971 by Mr. Prakash Shah, is a well-established name in the heating products and heat-treatment industry, with over five decades of rich industry experience. The company is now led by his two sons, who serve as partners and continue to build upon the strong foundation and legacy established by their father.

importers and stockists of high-quality heating products, specializing in heating wires and their various grades. With extensive knowledge of the industry and a strong understanding of diverse temperature requirements, we offer a comprehensive range of heating wires suitable for a wide variety of industrial applications. Our expertise and product range have established us as market leaders in heating wires, catering to the specific requirements of customers across different industries.

Prakash Electronics has been an authorized agent for Spark Brand products and their various grades for over 25 years. Our long-standing association, combined with our technical expertise and deep understanding of heat-treatment applications, has enabled us to build lasting relationships with customers across India. We proudly serve and supply materials to leading furnace manufacturers and other prominent industry players.

With our strong market presence, extensive distribution network, and in-depth understanding of the Indian market, we aim to showcase the latest products, technologies, and solutions from our principal, Beijing Shougang Gitane New Materials Corporation. Through our participation in this exhibition, we seek to present our comprehensive range of products to buyers, distributors, furnace manufacturers, and industry professionals while exploring new business opportunities and establishing long-term partnerships.

Our dedicated team of experienced professionals follows a client-first approach, ensuring prompt and reliable service at every stage—from immediate dispatch of materials to providing technical assistance and guidance on the selection of appropriate grades and products. With our strong supply capabilities and distribution network, we are equipped to serve customers on a pan-India basis.

With a legacy spanning more than five decades, Prakash Electronics continues to combine industry experience, technical expertise, quality products, and customer-focused service to remain a trusted partner in the heating and heat-treatment industry.
